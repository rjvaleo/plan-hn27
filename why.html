Why me.

The gap, plainly

I have led delivery from the client and vendor-coordination side, not from inside a model provider. And I have never been accountable for a consumption or revenue number.

The nearest I have come to the commercial side is agency work: estimates, statements of work, staffing plans and change orders. That is pricing a project, not owning a forecast. I would learn the second from the account teams, and I would not pretend to it in a review.

My titles have been Scrum Master, Agile Coach and Release Train Engineer. What I bring is the plan, the gates and the honest board, and a habit of reading everything first. This site is that habit, done in a day.
